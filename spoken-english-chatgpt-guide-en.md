# Improving Spoken English with ChatGPT or AI

## Purpose
Use ChatGPT as a daily speaking coach, not just a question-answer tool.

## Daily Strategy
1. Set a clear goal (job interviews, freelancing, daily conversation).
2. Practice every day for 20–30 minutes.
3. Ask for one question at a time.
4. Answer in English.
5. Request corrections for grammar, vocabulary, and natural expressions.
6. Repeat the corrected answer aloud.
7. Learn 5–10 new words from the conversation.
8. End with homework.

## Useful Prompts
- Act as my personal spoken English coach.
- Correct every mistake and explain why.
- Rewrite my answer like a native speaker.
- Ask only one question at a time.
- Give me pronunciation tips.
- Simulate a job interview or client meeting.

## Top 1% User Strategies
- They practice daily instead of studying occasionally.
- They use realistic conversations.
- They ask for detailed feedback.
- They review old mistakes.
- They focus on one domain (work, interviews, travel).
- They shadow corrected answers aloud.
- They use voice conversations when possible.
- They measure progress weekly.

## Weekly Routine
- Mon–Fri: 20–30 min conversation.
- Sat: Review mistakes.
- Sun: Mock interview or free conversation.

## Mindset
Consistency beats perfection.
